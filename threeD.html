Automaton Dancer

2025 | Made in Blender


Automaton Dancer is a 3D modeled, rendered and animated video of a mechanical ballerina being assembled. It seeks to convey the conflation between human and nonhuman work, as well as emphasizing how many overlook the skill and discipline ballet requires.
