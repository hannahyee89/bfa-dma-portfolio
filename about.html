About Me

I'm Hannah, a DMA student at SJSU. I'm primarily interested in 2D and 3D art.
-=-=-=-=-=-=-
A lot of my work features fish and outer space, as you might have noticed. I aim to include an element of etherealness in some of my pieces, and humor in others. I find that an ethereal style reminds me of a dream or a vague memory, which is visually and conceptually interesting to me. In addition, humor is always engaging in artwork, and I'm always apt to include it in my own art.
When I was taking ART 74, I became increasingly more interested in DMA and its range of mediums. I am excited about making something that captures the spirit of DMA.
