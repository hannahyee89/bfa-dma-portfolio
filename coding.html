Coding Works

Pet the Cat!

2025 | Made with HTML

​In this HTML narrative piece, the user goes through a "Choose Your Own Adventure" story, albeit a very short one. The purpose of this work is to convey a small, whimsical story that is both structured and nonsensical. I think interactive art, such as this project, is particularly intriguing since it engages the viewer in a more involved way.

Mostly Normal Drawing Program

2025 | Made with p5.js (via Processing)

This drawing tool is a medium for the user's own input. It features the typical-type brushes, as well as stamps I drew digitally. Art is not barred to a specific group of people, and I really enjoy seeing art other people have made, regardless of skill level. This drawing tool helps emphasize this sentiment.​ This was made in the Processing program, used in ART 74.

Let's go Fishing!

An interactive environment with sardines and tropical fish wearing rainbow propellor hats. Use the mouse to look around!
