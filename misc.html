Chair Empire

2026 | Made in Blender & Weebly

I worked with my brother, Calvin, to make Chair Empire (for an Art 2 project on collaboration). It is a website that is a fake online store selling impractical chairs. The conventional design of Chair Empire’s layout implies there is a market for useless chairs, and a market for useless chairs implies that people are spending money on them. Essentially, Chair Empire is a critique of poor financial decisions regarding ugly luxury items.
